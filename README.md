# Satisfaction Spa – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Satisfaction Spa is a hotel-based spa and wellness centre located at Grand Excelsior Hotel, Deira, Dubai. It offers a range of traditional massage therapies and relaxation treatments, including Arabic, Thai, Swedish, Royal, Four Hands, Hot Stone, Aromatherapy, and Moroccan Bath experiences.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting Satisfaction Spa as a welcoming wellness destination, making its treatments easier to explore and providing clearer pathways for customers to enquire and arrange appointments.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Homepage Positioning and First Impression
- Introduced a clearer opening presentation highlighting Satisfaction Spa, its wellness offering, and its Grand Excelsior Hotel location.
- Improved the messaging hierarchy to communicate the spa's identity and services more effectively.
- Created a more engaging introduction that helps visitors understand the business immediately.

### 2. Improved Treatment Discovery and Service Presentation
- Restructured the treatment presentation into a more organised and accessible service overview.
- Improved the visual separation and presentation of individual massage and wellness treatments.
- Made it easier for visitors to identify available experiences and explore relevant services.

### 3. More Accessible Booking and Contact Pathways
- Increased the visibility of appointment and enquiry options throughout the homepage.
- Improved the placement of contact information and enquiry calls to action.
- Made the contact journey more straightforward, helping visitors move from exploring treatments to making an enquiry.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Applied the approved colour palette of navy (#0F172A), gold (#DCA54A), and white to create a refined spa-focused appearance.
- **Typography:** Established a clearer typographic hierarchy for headings, descriptions, and supporting content.
- **Layout:** Improved section organisation, spacing, alignment, and content flow.
- **User Experience:** Made treatment information and important contact pathways easier to locate.
- **Consistency:** Applied a more cohesive visual language across homepage sections, including colours, typography, buttons, and content presentation.
- **Overall Appearance:** Developed a more polished and professional homepage that better reflects the spa's wellness positioning.
